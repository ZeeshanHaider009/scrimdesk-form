How do players register?

On Home, open a tournament's ⋮ menu and tap Share poster & link. Players open the link, fill in their team and tap Send registration. The team then shows up under Pending in your app.

Registrations aren't arriving

Open ScrimDesk while you are online: it fetches new registrations then. In the background, your phone decides when ScrimDesk may check, so it can take a while. To be told about new teams, allow notifications for ScrimDesk in your phone's Settings.

Payments

ScrimDesk never handles money. Entry fees go directly between players and organizers; the app only shows the payment screenshot a player sends so you can check it.

Deleting your data

Deleting a tournament also deletes any of its registrations still waiting on the server. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Players who want a registration removed can ask the organizer, or email us.
