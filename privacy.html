Privacy policy

ScrimDesk: Tournament Manager · Last updated 10 October 2026

In short

ScrimDesk: Tournament Manager has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We never see it.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your organizer profile (name, WhatsApp number and an optional logo), your tournaments, and the registrations you add: team name, captain name, captain WhatsApp number, player game IDs, payment screenshots and team logos. It is stored in the app's private storage.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, unless you choose to share. When you send a poster, a group link or a backup file, it goes through WhatsApp or another app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy. The poster's QR code contains the tournament details, the payment number you entered and your WhatsApp number, so players can register with you.

The player registration page

Players fill in a small web page (zeeshanhaider009.github.io/scrimdesk). It runs only in the player's browser: it sends nothing to us, and the payment screenshot stays on the player's phone until they share it with you on WhatsApp. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy policy.

Backups

Backup files you create stay wherever you save or send them. If your phone's own backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), it may include ScrimDesk's data under your account.

Permissions

Photos: only the pictures you pick (logos and payment screenshots). On iPhone, saving a poster asks to add a photo to your library. On Android 8–9, saving a poster asks for storage access. Nothing else.

Money

ScrimDesk never handles payments. Entry fees go directly between players and organizers.

Children

ScrimDesk is made for tournament organizers and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete all data removes everything from this phone. Uninstalling the app does too.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact on the ScrimDesk page in Google Play or the App Store.
