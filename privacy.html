Privacy policy

ScrimDesk: Tournament Manager · Last updated 14 October 2026

In short

ScrimDesk: Tournament Manager has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your tournaments and teams stay on your phone. The only thing that passes through a server is a player's registration, on its way from the registration page to the organizer's app, and it is deleted from the server as soon as the app has it.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your organizer profile (name, WhatsApp number and an optional logo), your tournaments and their posters, and the registrations: team name and tag, the name and WhatsApp number of the player who registered the team, players' in-game names and game IDs, payment screenshots and team logos. It is stored in the app's private storage.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, unless you choose to share. When you send a poster or a group link, it goes through WhatsApp or another app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy. The registration link you share points to your tournament. So that the registration page can show it, the app keeps the tournament's details in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) while it is online: its name, game, start time, number of teams, entry fee and prize pool (if any), your payment method, payment number and the account holder's name (if you set them) and your country's dialing code. Anyone who has the link can see them. They are removed together with the tournament's online record, a day after the tournament starts or when you delete it.

The player registration page

Players fill in a small web page (scrimdesk.web.app). When they tap Send registration, what they entered (team name and tag, their name and WhatsApp number, players' names and game IDs, the payment screenshot and an optional team logo) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) for that tournament only. The organizer's ScrimDesk app downloads it and then deletes it from Firestore; registrations the app never collects are removed a day after the tournament starts. Only the organizer's app can read them. Google processes this data under the Firebase terms and privacy policy. The page itself is hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google), which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under Google's privacy policy.

How the app connects

To fetch registrations, the app signs in to Firebase anonymously: there is no account, email or password, only a random ID that marks which tournaments belong to this phone. Nothing else in the app is sent anywhere.

Phone backups

If your phone's own backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), it may include ScrimDesk's data under your account.

Permissions

Photos: only the pictures you pick (posters, logos and payment screenshots), and on iPhone adding a poster to your Photos when you choose Save Image. Notifications: to tell you when a new team registers. Internet: to download registrations from the registration page. Nothing else.

Money

ScrimDesk never handles payments. Entry fees go directly between players and organizers.

Children

ScrimDesk is made for tournament organizers and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on this phone. Deleting a tournament in the app also deletes any of its registrations still waiting on the server. Players who want a registration removed can ask the organizer to delete it.
